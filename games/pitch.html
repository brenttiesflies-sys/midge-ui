How this Pitch works (tap)

Goal

Be first to 11 points. Each hand, four players are dealt six cards. You bid for how many of the four points you can take; the winner ("the pitcher") names trump and leads.

Bidding

Going clockwise from the dealer's left, each player bids 2, 3, 4, or passes — each bid must beat the last. The dealer may steal by matching the top bid. If everyone passes, the dealer is forced to bid 2. Highest bidder picks trump.

Playing

The pitcher leads first. You must follow the suit led if you can — but you may trump at any time even when you could follow a plain suit. If a trump is led, you must play trump if you hold one. Highest trump wins the trick; with no trump, the highest card of the suit led wins.

The four points

High — highest trump in play. Low — lowest trump in play. Jack — the Jack of trump (only if it was dealt). Each goes to whoever captures that card. Game — most card-pip value taken: 10=10, A=4, K=3, Q=2, J=1. Ties award no Game point.

Scoring

Everyone keeps the points they take. But if the pitcher fails to make their bid, they're set back — the bid is subtracted, and their score can go negative ("in the hole"). Points count in order High, Low, Jack, Game, so if two players hit 11 the same hand, the pitcher wins ties.
