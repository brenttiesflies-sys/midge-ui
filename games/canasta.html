How this Canasta works (tap)

Goal

Score the most points over multiple hands, first to 5000. You score by melding cards and completing canastas (melds of 7+).

Your turn

1 · Draw 2 from the stock, or take the whole discard pile if you can immediately meld its top card. 2 · Meld new sets or add to existing ones (optional). 3 · Discard one card to end your turn.

Melds

3+ cards of the same rank. Wilds (jokers & 2s) can fill in, max 2 per meld, and natural cards must always outnumber wilds. A canasta is 7 cards: clean/red (no wilds) = 500, dirty/black (1–2 wilds) = 300.

First meld minimum

Your first meld of a hand must total at least: 50 pts (score <1500), 90 (1500–2999), 120 (3000+). Card values: joker 50, 2/ace 20, 8–K 10, 4–7 & black 3 = 5.

Threes

Red 3 — bonus card. It's auto-played face-up for 100 pts (800 if you get all four) and you draw a replacement. Black 3 — discarding one freezes/blocks the pile so the next player can't take it. Black 3s can only be melded as you go out.

Frozen pile

If a wild or red 3 is in the pile, it's frozen: you can only take it by holding 2 natural cards matching the top card.

Going out

You need 2 canastas completed. Then meld/discard your last card to go out for a +100 bonus and end the hand.
