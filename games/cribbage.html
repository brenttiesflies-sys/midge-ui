How cribbage works (tap to read)

Cribbage is a race to 121 points, scored on the board with pegs. You score points two ways each hand: during the play, and in the show (counting your hand at the end).

1 · The deal

Each player gets 6 cards. You each throw 2 away into the crib — a bonus 4-card hand that belongs to the dealer. The deal alternates every hand, so the crib does too.

2 · The starter

One card is cut from the deck. It counts toward everyone's hand and the crib in the show. If it's a Jack, the dealer pegs 2 ("his heels").

3 · The play (pegging)

You take turns laying cards, adding up a running count. You can't go past 31. Score as you play:

• Make the count exactly 15 → 2 points
• Make it exactly 31 → 2 points
• Match the previous card (pair) → 2; three of a kind → 6; four → 12
• Complete a run of 3+ (any order) → 1 per card
• Can't play without busting? Say Go. Opponent pegs 1, or plays on.
• Last card laid → 1 point.

Face cards count as 10. Ace counts as 1.

4 · The show

Now everyone counts their 4 cards + the starter. Non-dealer counts first (matters near 121). Then dealer counts their hand, then their crib.

• Every combo summing to 15 → 2
• Each pair → 2
• Runs → 1 per card
• Flush → 4 (all 4 hand cards same suit), 5 if the starter matches too
• Nobs → Jack in hand matching the starter's suit → 1

First to 121 wins instantly — even mid-count.
