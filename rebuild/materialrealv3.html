What it is

The most prized of the North Country soft hackles. The plover's small breast and neck feathers carry fine, mobile barbs that breathe and pulse in moving water — the lifelike motion that makes spider patterns deadly on a swing. Traditional dressings, traced back through Pritt and Fogg, call for the speckled flank for its subtle barring.
