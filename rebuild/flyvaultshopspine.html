About this piece

Bobbins turned from a variety of woods and finished by hand. The thread rides on a Singer Class 66 sewing-machine bobbin, tensioned through a rubber washer and a flat knurled nut. Each painted version is finished in a brook-trout scheme and sealed in epoxy — no two are alike.

FlyVault connects you to the maker. Price, payment and shipping are arranged directly between you and Christian.
